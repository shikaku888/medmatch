# How other health apps handle FDA / disclaimer (observed)
- WebMD / Healthline: verbatim FDA at article footer (small, gray), not sticky; consent via terms link.
- Drugs.com: light banner at result with source links; first-launch consent checkbox.
- MyFitnessPal: disclaimer in Settings → About + light footer on reports.
- Medscape / Epocrates: "Not a substitute for professional advice" small at result screen; no overlay on scan/home.
- Pattern: disclosure at point of use (result) + first-launch acknowledgment; never hidden, never oversized on non-result screens.
- Western-user trust driver: visible evidence sources (FDA, NIH, DOI) + clear trust score, not absence of disclaimer.
