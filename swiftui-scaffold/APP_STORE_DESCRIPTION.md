# App Store Description (EN — US target)
Title: MedMatch — Supplement & Drug Interaction Reference
Subtitle: Reference-only health checker from FDA, NIH, and open literature.

Description:
MedMatch provides automated reference information about supplement, medication, and food interactions using structured public databases (DailyMed / FDA labels, SUPP.AI DOIs, NIH DSLD, openFDA, RxNorm, PubChem). It is NOT a diagnosis, treatment, or medical prescription tool. Always confirm with a licensed physician or pharmacist.

Features:
- Scan barcode or enter manually; local cabinet stays on your device (no account, no tracking)
- Evidence-based interaction list with source citations; CYP-inferred pairs clearly labeled "Not clinician-verified"
- Timing notes (separate doses); nutrient depletion watch; Beers criteria reminders
- Print / save PDF report with FDA disclaimer
- Subscription: Pro ($19/mo unlimited) · Caregiver ($69/mo profiles); restore anytime in App Settings

Privacy:
No tracking. Your cabinet is stored locally (SwiftData). Lookup data is fetched stateless from public sources; no user meds or profile are transmitted to our servers.

Medical Disclaimer (VERBATIM per 21 CFR §101.93):
"This statement has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Information above is an automated reference from public databases — not medical advice. Consult a licensed physician or pharmacist."
